Spatial Acoustic Engine & Cultural Heritage Architecture

How AetherSpace combines W3C Web Audio spatialisation with digital storytelling research for headset-free immersion.

Web Audio API 3D Spatial Signal Graph

Headset-Free Spatial Immersion

Built on native browser Web Audio API PannerNode and AudioListener coordinates, enabling real-time 3D sound localisation, elevation, and directional cones without headsets or native installs.

Binaural Presence & Narrative

Informed by spatial audio journalism and oral storytelling research, the dynamic Story Guide aligns layered environmental scenes and proximity cues so listeners step inside living cultural spaces.

Sri Lankan Cultural & Natural Heritage

Brings together Sinharaja's endemic Purple-faced Langur and Blue Magpie, Anuradhapura's 1982 UNESCO sacred stupas and hydraulic tanks, and Galle Dutch Fort's 1988 UNESCO multicultural ramparts.

Sources & Grounding: Web Audio API & PannerNode Spatialisation Guides (MDN Web Docs); Spatial Audio Designer (Polys & Bendelac, Virginia Tech, Web3D ’22); Spatial Audio and Immersive Journalism (Vicente & Pérez-Seijo, Profesional de la información, 2022); Foundations of Interactive Sound Design for Traditional Storytelling (Wyse & Subramanian, NUS); Empowering Voices, Shaping Futures: Digital Storytelling for Sustainable Transformation (Funk, 2024); Real-Time Sound Mapping in Augmented Reality Using Web Browser Technologies (Vlad & Buraga, WEBIST 2025); Immersive Web Experiences Without Headsets (Brill Creations); Sri Lankan regional heritage audio transcripts (Anuradhapura, Galle Dutch Fort) & Sinharaja forest field notes (Chercheur de sons).
